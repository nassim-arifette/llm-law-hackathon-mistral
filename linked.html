Words and money, linked without the content

A transfer leaves a customer's account. The next morning she asks the bank's assistant why. Every record is sealed in two parts: a private body that stays at the bank, and a small header that carries a case tag and the fingerprints of the records it refers to. Counsel receives only the headers, and can still prove what belongs together, what answered what, and in which order.
